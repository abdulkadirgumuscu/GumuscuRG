Abdülkadir Gümüşçü is an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Harran University and an Honorary Senior Lecturer in the Department of Bioengineering at Imperial College London. He leads the Gumuscu Research Group, which develops machine learning, computer vision and optimization methods for health, agriculture and energy: from Parkinson's disease diagnosis and plant disease detection to automatic inspection of solar modules.

He joined Harran University as a Research Assistant in 2009 and received his PhD there in 2016 with a thesis on hybrid feature selection in high-dimensional data clusters. His MSc thesis at Gaziantep University (2012) addressed video anomaly detection using morphological processing. Alongside his research, he is active in technology transfer as Deputy Manager of Sanliurfa Technopark.
