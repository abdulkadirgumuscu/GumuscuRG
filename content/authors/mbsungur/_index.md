Mehmet Burak Sungur is an MSc student in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Harran University. He received his BSc in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Manisa Celal Bayar University in 2015.

His thesis research forecasts the weekly power generation of 77 solar power plants located across different parts of Şanlıurfa province, Türkiye, using machine learning and data mining methods. The study also analyses plant performance and reveals how climate and location factors shape power generation.
