Mustafa Kenan Yurtçu is a PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Harran University. His doctoral research focuses on disease diagnosis from medical data using machine learning and deep learning methods, covering 2D medical images and 1D ExG signals for tasks such as classification and segmentation, including recent approaches like graph neural networks (GNNs).

Before joining the group, he worked on open-source information security tools such as GLPI and PacketFence, with a focus on network intrusion detection. During his MSc in Chemistry at Hacettepe University, he studied the mathematical modelling of electrochemical cells, including batteries and fuel cells, with a long-term interest in analysing SEM/EDX images. Across these areas, his work builds on up-to-date machine learning and deep learning algorithms.
