At a TUBITAK-supported event at Sanliurfa Technopark, Dr. Abdulkadir Gumuscu gave a talk on "Developing Entrepreneurial Culture in Engineering Students with Innovative Technologies".

The talk focused on how engineering students can use emerging technologies to turn their projects into products, and on the support available to young entrepreneurs through technoparks.
