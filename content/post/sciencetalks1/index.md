Dr. Abdulkadir Gumuscu was a speaker in the TUBITAK Science Talks series at Viranşehir Science and Art Center, with a session on "Artificial Intelligence and Entrepreneurship".

He shared examples of how artificial intelligence is used to solve real problems and encouraged students to think about the ideas they could build themselves.
